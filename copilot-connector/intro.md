## Snaplii

This connector lets Copilot Studio agents browse Snaplii gift card brands, check the user's Snaplii Cash balance and get a price quote before buying. Spending comes only from the user's prepaid Snaplii Cash; a quote charges nothing.

### Prerequisites

A Snaplii account in Canada or the United States and an API key created in the Snaplii app (More, Payment Methods, AI Payment Management, New API Key).

### How to get credentials

Create the API key in the Snaplii app, choose its scope and spending limit, and enter it when you create the connection. The key can be revoked in the app at any time.

### Get started with your connector

List the categories and brands, open a brand's details to pick a card and amount, then quote that card.

### Known issues and limitations

- Available for Snaplii accounts in Canada and the United States only.
- The connector reads the catalog, the balance and quotes; it does not buy gift cards, pay bills or send money.
- The catalog lists the brands shown on the Snaplii app home page.
- Spending comes only from prepaid Snaplii Cash.
- The card ID in a quote must be copied exactly from the brand details.
